Motion card interaction references
----------------------------------
The portfolio's card presentation and shared card/detail transition adapt
these publicly accessible examples to vanilla JavaScript and native dialog:
https://motion.dev/examples/vue-animate-view-app-store
https://motion.dev/examples/js-shared-view-animation
https://motion.dev/examples/js-gestures
The underlying Motion library license is listed above. No Motion+ source
was accessed or bundled. The portfolio retains its own content and imagery.

Generated project cover images
