Brand artwork removal
----------------------
The current distribution contains no MyBatis, HAProxy, Devicon product-logo,
or NAVER Cloud promotional image files. Technology names remain descriptive
text; software copyright licenses are not treated as trademark permissions.
For MyBatis, the official About page identifies Apache-2.0 for source code
and documentation but did not establish logo reuse terms in this review:
https://blog.mybatis.org/p/about.html?m=1
Reviewed: 2026-11-16. The former logo was replaced by data-mapper.svg without
asserting that the prior use was unlawful. The other product logos were also
replaced as a conservative design choice, not as a finding of infringement.
Earlier Git revisions have their own asset and license records.
